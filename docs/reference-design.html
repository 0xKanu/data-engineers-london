About Us

Data Engineers London is a community-driven group dedicated to bringing together data professionals, engineers, architects, and enthusiasts across the capital. We provide a space to share knowledge, discuss best practices, and explore the latest technologies shaping the modern data stack.

Whether you're building massive data pipelines, architecting real-time streaming platforms, or just starting your journey in data engineering, you'll find a welcoming community ready to learn and grow together.
