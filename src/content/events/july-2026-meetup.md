## Talk 1 — Streaming Data Pipelines at Scale

Sarah will walk through how her team migrated from batch to streaming processing, covering event-time semantics, state management, and exactly-once guarantees.

## Talk 2 — Running Flink on Kubernetes

Marcus shares practical lessons from running production Flink workloads on Kubernetes, including resource management, checkpointing strategies, and monitoring.
